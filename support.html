Talverik support

Questions, setup help and how to reach us.

For app questions, email talverik.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Talverik version, what you expected and what happened. Remove private details from screenshots.

This mailbox is not an emergency service and is not monitored for urgent help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Getting ready offline

Open the guides, map and tools before leaving connectivity. Check that the files you need are installed and marked ready. The world overview is not a detailed local map. Detailed map and model availability depends on the build; obtain any needed files while you have internet access.

AI setup

AI is optional and needs a supported iPhone, installed model files and enough free storage. The app reports compatibility and setup problems. If AI is unavailable, you can still read the guides. Its questions and answers are processed on the phone; an answer can still be wrong.

Maps and location

Allow Location access in iPhone Settings when you want your position or track recording. Recording may continue with the screen locked until you stop it. A location or compass reading can be inaccurate; compare it with your surroundings. Maps and tracks do not establish a safe route.

Backups

Open Settings → Backup. Use Back up now, or choose an automatic schedule and a folder. Automatic copies run when the app opens and a backup is due. Check the last successful copy before changing phones or removing the app.

Turn on Protect with password before making copies that need password encryption. We cannot recover a forgotten password. A document’s Face ID lock does not password-encrypt an exported backup. Keep older copies until you have checked a restore.

Documents and permissions

Use Tools → Documents to scan or import a file. If the optional lock is enabled, iOS asks for Face ID or your device passcode. Camera, microphone, speech and notification permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings; the related feature needs its permission.

Protect private information

Do not email passwords, medical records, full backups or unredacted identity documents. Support cannot see your phone’s records or recover them remotely. Read the Privacy Policy for backups, sharing and support-email handling.

How to use Talverik

Kits

How do I create a kit?

Prepare → Add kit, then choose a ready-made list (go bag, car, home…) or an empty one. You can change the items later.

How do I count what I have?

In a kit, tap − or + next to an item; hold it to count quickly. Tap the number to type it.

How do I put items inside another?

Swipe the item to the right and choose Add items inside — for example, a first aid kit inside a go bag.

How do I track expiry dates?

Open the item → Details → Expiry. Expiring items show in the kits overview; turn on reminders in Settings → Reminders.

How do I find an item by its barcode?

Prepare → the barcode button at the top, then point the camera at the package.

How do I move a list from a spreadsheet?

In a kit, ⋯ → Import table (CSV). Export table saves the list as a table.

Readiness and documents

What is Phone readiness?

A checklist of iPhone settings for emergencies: permissions, Emergency SOS, Medical ID, Find My and alerts. Tap a row to open that setting.

How do I keep a copy of my passport?

Tools → Documents → + → Scan document. Lock documents with Face ID in the same + menu.

Map and tracks

How do I download my region's map?

Map → the maps button → Offline maps → your country → your region.

How do I save a place?

Touch and hold the map, then give the place a name and a category.

How do I record a track?

Map → Record track. It keeps recording with the screen off; for reliability allow Location "Always" for Talverik.

How do I go back along a track?

Tracks → the track → Guide to the start of the track. An arrow and the distance appear at the top of the map.

What is true north in the compass?

With Location allowed, the compass corrects for the difference between magnetic and geographic north.

AI and signals

How should I ask the AI?

Short and specific: "How do I purify water?", "What goes in a go bag?". You can also speak — tap the microphone.

How do I signal with light or sound?

Tools → Morse → Flashlight or Sound → the large button. It works while the screen is open.

Questions and answers

Does Talverik work without the internet?

Yes. Kits, guides, documents, the compass, tracks and downloaded maps work with no network. The internet is needed only to download your region's map and the AI once, and to check for a new version.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no analytics, no servers of ours. Data leaves the phone only when you send a backup or a document yourself.

How do I keep my data safe?

Turn on automatic backup in Settings → Backup and choose a folder in iCloud Drive, so copies survive losing the phone. You can protect backups with a password.

I forgot my backup password.

No one can recover it, including us. A protected backup cannot be opened without its password.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Make a backup on the old iPhone, then choose Restore on the new one.

Why are the AI and maps downloaded separately?

They are large (the AI about 1.5 GB, a region's map up to 1 GB), so you download only what you need, when you want.

How far can I trust the AI?

It is a small model running on the phone. Answers marked "From the guide" come from the guides; other answers are general and can be wrong. In danger, call your emergency number first.

Why is there no first aid in the app?

Version 1.0 leaves it out on purpose: inaccurate medical advice is dangerous. For medical questions the AI gives your emergency number. Take a first-aid course with the Red Cross or a local provider.

Why is there no SOS button?

Your iPhone's built-in Emergency SOS is more reliable, and on newer models it works via satellite. The guide on preparing your iPhone shows how to set it up.

Why does the map show miles or kilometres?

It follows the country chosen in Talverik's settings: the US uses miles and feet, the UK miles and yards, the others kilometres.

What is Night red?

A red screen for the dark that keeps your night vision. Turn it on with the moon button on the map or in the compass.

How do I contact you?

Write to talverik.support@gmail.com.
