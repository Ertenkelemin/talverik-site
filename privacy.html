Talverik Privacy Policy

What Talverik keeps, where, and what it never collects.

Policy version: 17 November 2026.

Talverik is provided by the individual developer identified as the seller on its App Store listing. For privacy questions, contact talverik.support@gmail.com.

The app

Talverik has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not collect your personal data through the app, sell it or use it for advertising. Your records are processed on your iPhone.

Kits, document scans and recognized text, imported files, saved places, recorded tracks, personal and medical details you enter, settings and AI conversations remain on the device unless you choose to export, share or back them up. The AI processes questions and answers locally and does not read your personal records. Document text recognition is local too.

Permissions

  • Location shows your position, coordinates, compass direction and saved places. Recording a track can continue while the screen is locked or the app is in the background, until you stop recording. Location is not sent to us.
  • The camera scans documents and product barcodes when you choose to scan.
  • Optional microphone and speech recognition access turns a spoken AI question into text on the device. If on-device recognition is unavailable, the app does not fall back to cloud transcription.
  • Optional notifications deliver local reminders.
  • The optional document lock uses iOS authentication, including Face ID or the device passcode. Talverik does not receive your face template.
  • File and contact pickers give access to items you select, for import or export.

You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Denying a permission affects the feature that needs it.

Backups and sharing

Backups are saved to a folder you choose, which can be on the iPhone, in iCloud Drive or with another file provider. Automatic backups run when the app opens and a copy is due; they are not a guaranteed background schedule. Backups can include documents and personal information; AI chats are included when you enable that option. A safety copy made before a restore includes chats so the current state can be recovered.

Turn on Protect with password to encrypt new backup files. Without that setting, app-exported backups are not password-encrypted by Talverik. The password is kept in this iPhone’s keychain; we do not have it and cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Turning protection off does not unlock older protected files.

iOS device backup and your chosen file provider may also store copies under their own settings and privacy policies. Downloaded maps and AI models are excluded from iOS backup and are not included in the app’s exported backups. Protect files you share: the receiving app or person controls the resulting copy.

Network use and Apple services

The production app uses the network for Apple-hosted map and model downloads when available, and to check the App Store for a newer app version. Those requests do not contain your kits, documents, location, tracks or AI conversations. Apple receives the connection information needed to serve requests, such as an IP address, and handles its services under its own privacy policy.

If you choose Directions in Maps, the destination is handed to Apple Maps, which operates under Apple’s privacy terms. Other exports and links open only when you choose them. Talverik does not upload your records to a developer-operated server.

Keeping and deleting data

Your records remain until you remove them or delete the app. Copies in Files, cloud storage, device backups, other apps or with recipients can remain after app deletion; remove those separately if needed. Because we do not hold your app records, we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

Support and this website

If you email talverik.support@gmail.com, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer and resolve the request, retain them only as needed for support or legal obligations, and delete them on request when permitted. Email is handled by our email provider; do not send passwords, medical records or full backups.

These static web pages contain no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, scripts or external fonts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and secure the pages. Email and web hosting are separate from on-device app processing.

Changes and contact

We will update the policy version when these practices change. For questions or support-email deletion requests, write to talverik.support@gmail.com.
